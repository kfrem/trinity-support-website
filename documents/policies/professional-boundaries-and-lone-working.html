Professional Boundaries and Lone Working

Owner: Operations Management Approved: 3 June 2026 Review: 3 June 2027 Version: 2026.1

Safe relationships, spending time alone, transport, home visits and recording boundaries.

Purpose

  • This policy sets out how Trinity Support Limited delivers safe, lawful and person-centred 18+ supported accommodation in relation to professional boundaries and lone working.
  • Where a placement is for a looked-after child or care leaver aged 16 or 17, the service must also follow the Supported Accommodation (England) Regulations 2023, Ofsted registration requirements and the SCCIF supported accommodation inspection framework.
  • The service does not present ordinary tenancy support as regulated personal care. If personal care, nursing care or a regulated activity is proposed, management must obtain specialist advice and confirm whether CQC registration is required before the service starts.

Responsible Lead

  • Policy owner: Operations Management.
  • Executive oversight: the senior management team, with delegated evidence control through the policy portal.
  • All staff must know where this policy is stored, how to print it, and which form or register records compliance.

Practice Standard

  • Safe relationships, spending time alone, transport, home visits and recording boundaries.
  • Support must be planned with the resident, recorded in plain English and reviewed when needs, risk, accommodation, staffing or legal status changes.
  • Residents must be treated as adults with rights, privacy and choice. Staff must balance independence with safeguarding, tenancy conditions and the safety of others.
  • Decisions must be evidence-led, proportionate, non-discriminatory and capable of external scrutiny by commissioners, local authorities, auditors or regulators.

Procedure

  • Identify the issue, need or request and check the resident's current support plan, risk assessment and tenancy or licence agreement.
  • Discuss the matter with the resident wherever safe and practicable, record their wishes and explain any limits clearly.
  • Agree proportionate actions, timescales and the person responsible. Escalate immediately where there is safeguarding, health and safety, criminal, financial or regulatory risk.
  • Record the decision on the relevant form or register on the same working day. Attach supporting evidence such as photographs, correspondence, professional advice or resident feedback where appropriate.
  • Review the outcome with the resident and update the support plan, risk assessment, property record, staff supervision note or improvement plan as required.

Records and Evidence

  • Current policy version, review date and owner.
  • Relevant completed forms, case notes, audit logs, risk assessments and management sign-off.
  • Evidence of resident involvement, equality considerations, escalation and learning from incidents or complaints.

Review and Escalation

  • This policy is reviewed at least annually by Operations Management and the relevant functional lead, or earlier after a serious incident, complaint, safeguarding referral, commissioner feedback or regulatory update.
  • Any gap that could affect resident safety, legal compliance or inspection evidence must be placed on the Quality Improvement Plan within two working days.

Source Awareness

This document is aligned to current supported accommodation, safeguarding, housing governance and regulated-activity awareness. It must still be adapted to actual contracts, properties, resident needs and commissioned service specifications.
